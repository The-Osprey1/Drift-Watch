DRIFT sync

Sets your Drift-Watch's time, time zone, active watch faces and Wi-Fi over Bluetooth. Nothing is installed and nothing leaves this page except what you send to the watch.

This browser can't use Bluetooth

On an iPhone or iPad, open this page in the free Bluefy app. On Android, Windows, macOS, Linux or a Chromebook, use Chrome or Edge. The page must be opened over https.

1. Open phone sync on the watch

  1. Swipe up on the watch face to open Settings.
  2. Find Phone sync (page 2) and tap it. The watch shows its name, such as Drift-1A2B, for 3 minutes.

Windows users: Connect directly from this page. Do not pair the watch in Windows Bluetooth Settings (if paired, click "Remove device" in Windows Settings first, or Windows will disconnect the link every second).

3. Active watch faces (Hot swap)

Choose which faces you want active when swiping left or right on the watch. Selecting just two enables fast hot-swap switching between your favorites.

4. Wi-Fi (optional)

Lets the watch keep itself right over the internet. It only works with 2.4 GHz networks. The password goes straight to the watch, over a Bluetooth link that is not encrypted, so only do this with the watch close to you.

Using an iPhone Personal Hotspot? Turn on Maximize Compatibility in Settings > Personal Hotspot so it broadcasts on 2.4 GHz, and keep that screen open while the watch joins.
